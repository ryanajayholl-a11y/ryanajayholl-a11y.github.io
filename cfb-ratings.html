2026 CFB Team Ratings

By Ryan Hollingshead
Updated Oct 25, 2026

Resume rating = what a team has accomplished, from wins and losses only (through week 8). Each win over an FBS team earns that opponent's AdjW% (win % × conference weight); each FBS loss costs 1.4 − the opponent's AdjW%; an FCS loss costs 1.9; an FCS win earns 0. The total is divided by games played.

Best win / Worst loss = win over the best-ranked opponent / loss to the worst-ranked opponent, with that opponent's resume rank. Prev = last week's resume rank (blank in the first week); ▲ green = moved up. Power Rating # = rank on the Power Ratings tab.

EXPTS = expected points per game from the model. Rk: offense 1 = most points scored; defense 1 = fewest allowed. Conf Rk = power rank within conference.

Δ ’25 = change vs. 2025 (column M). ▲ green = better (scoring more / allowing fewer), ▼ red = worse. Net Δ = offense Δ − defense Δ: the change in expected scoring margin.
